Theodore was born to Ainel Moreland and Eulalia Wyck. Both are of noble birth, his father especially came from a long line of nobles and royals for generations. They live in a secret kingdom tucked away in between Scotland and England, held together there by magic so that most normal human folks never find it. Should they stumble upon it, they either forget what they were doing or get lost in the place, never to return to their homeland. Their kingdom is called Mirenion has been around since before the days of Camelot, if such things are to be believed. The ancient king at that time knew of the strife that was going on between humans and magical folk and locked up their kingdom tight so that only those trusted within the family would ever be able to release the secret hold it had on the world.

Theodore, sometimes known as Theo in the regular world he is seen in, wasn't always called that. He was born as Anderl Oliver Morland, it was only when he decided to make his daring escape that he changed his name and started anew. What made him escape such a place that was considered beautiful and full of magic and life? His family. Well, not his family really: his father.

His parents had always wanted to have more children and with his younger sister Gracelyn being in the fragile state she was, his father insisted on his mother carrying again. Unfortunately, this time she didn't make it and because of that, King Ainel became more ruthless. Because there were no 'appropriate' heirs left (according to him), Anderl was forced into an arranged marriage with a noble woman he hardly knew. It wasn't as if he didn't want to marry, that wasn't the case at all. It was his father's fanatic way about it. Marry, have heirs, carry on the family line. Anderl tried to insist that there wasn't such a rush for that, he was only in his early twenties at that time, he didn't need to marry then. But Ainel wouldn't see reason. It was either marry or join the army and Anderl had no desire for either.

With that, he had packed some of his precious belongings and grabbed whatever coin he could keep on him, kissed his sister on the forehead as she had been sleeping and snuck away in the night. That was over ten years ago now. His father had never sent anyone to look for him, or at least he had assumed so. With the knowledge he had from his school lessons, he attending university in London and eventually started running his own bookshop. As far as anyone was concerned, he was an orphan, not a runaway prince with a forsaken crown.

He named his beloved bookshop The Wyck Chronicler after his mother and settled into life there easily. When he wasn't working, he was reading or traveling, enjoying as much of the world as he could without the tight restrictions of his family life back in Mirenion. Would he ever return to his home kingdom? He wasn't sure if he ever could do that, but a letter from his sister one day might just encourage him to go back. Until then, he was happy working at the bookshop and making a life and a new name for himself. No longer was he Anderl, but THEDORE OLIVER ATWELL instead.
